!
Understand the limits of a hash.

A matching SHA-256 digest does not, by itself, prove authorship, ownership, creation date, legal validity or originality. Those claims require independently trusted records, timestamps, signatures or other evidence. The timestamp in a downloaded receipt is generated by your device and is not a trusted timestamp authority.

ABOUT THE PROJECT

Preserve. Verify. Trust.

Legacy Mundial is a lightweight, browser-based utility for computing a digital file's SHA-256 fingerprint and comparing it with a reference value. The file is read locally by your browser; this page does not upload the selected file.

The tool is an integrity-checking aid. It is not a certification service, timestamp authority, digital-signature service or independent proof of authorship.
